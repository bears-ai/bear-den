Imported identities and account mappings

Only a verified Bear Admin can prepare or confirm reconnection and revocation. This page shows identity labels and provenance, never memory content.

Source labels and numeric account IDs are information, not permission. Nothing is matched automatically. Choose the person and a verified existing member of this Bear deliberately. Other people remain separate.

Reconnection lets the selected account recall this person's eligible personal memory. It does not grant membership, enable Work, share facts into core, or bypass other memory restrictions.

If the account already has an identity, confirmation requires a separate acknowledgement: that identity is parked with all records and relations preserved. No facts are lost or combined. A parked identity can be reconnected later through another review.
